# Yusuf Aiman — character spec (LOCKED)

Status: **reference only**. No production cut-outs, 3D model or rig exist yet. Changing anything below needs explicit approval from the owner.

Source of truth: `reference/guideline-board.png`, `reference/yusuf-cartoon.png`, and the Yusuf panels of `brand/reference/character-storytelling-board.png`.

## Role
Main host. Explainer, guide, narrator. Calm, analytical, friendly, confident. Breaks complex ideas down simply.

## Appearance
- Stylised 3D cartoon (smooth, Pixar-inspired), large head, rounded features, warm light skin tone.
- Hair: short, dark, modern wavy quiff; shorter on the sides.
- Face: dark simple eyes, expressive dark eyebrows, small nose, friendly smile, light goatee (signature).
- Signature outfit (default): plain black crew-neck T-shirt, black joggers, white sneakers.
- Accessory: grey/silver smartwatch on the left wrist (always present).

## Approved variations (from the guideline board)
Outfits: 01 Signature (default), 02 Hoodie, 03 Jacket, 04 Polo, 05 Shirt, 06 Tech Vest, 07 Lab Coat.
Expressions: 31 named emotions (1 Neutral … 31 Mind Blown) as listed on the guideline board.
Poses: Explain, Present, Point, Think, Ask, Idea, Discuss, Wave, Thumbs Up, Cross Arms, Hold Tablet, Hold Laptop, Read Book, Celebrate.

## Never
- Remove the goatee or the watch; change hair colour or style; add glasses (glasses belong to Aiman).
- Use a different art style (flat vector, anime, photoreal) for Yusuf in the same episode.
