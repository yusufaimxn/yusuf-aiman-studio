# Recording guide for Yusuf's voice samples

## Setup
- **Room:** quiet and soft (curtains, a sofa or a wardrobe of clothes help). Turn off fans and air-conditioning while recording.
- **Microphone:** use the same mic every session. A USB condenser or dynamic mic is fine. Avoid laptop and Bluetooth earphone mics.
- **Distance:** about 15 to 20 cm (a hand-span), slightly off-axis to avoid popping. Keep it the same every take.
- **Level:** loud parts should peak around -6 dB and never hit red. The enrollment UI meter turns red near clipping.
- **Format:** WAV at 44.1 or 48 kHz is best. MP3 at 192 kbps or higher is acceptable.
- **Processing:** turn off noise suppression, auto-gain and "voice enhancement" in the mic software.

## Delivery
- Speak the way you speak on your channel: Bahasa Melayu with your normal English mix. Don't put on a "presenter" voice.
- Record several styles across sessions: conversational, explaining, excited, calm, serious, humorous and curious.
- Keep energy natural. A voice clone copies what it hears, so exaggerated delivery gives an exaggerated clone.
- Leave small natural pauses, but trim long silences at the start and end of a take.
- Only your voice: no music, no other speakers, no TV in the background.

## How much
- **Instant clone (testing):** 1 to 2 minutes of your best clean audio in total.
- **Professional clone (production):** at least 30 minutes of approved audio, ideally 1 to 3 hours. Split it into takes of 1 to 5 minutes.

## Suggested reading material
- The prompts in the enrollment UI, which cover each style.
- Your own past scripts, read out naturally.
- Explanations of tech topics you know well, spoken off the cuff. These usually sound the most natural.
