Status: **reference only**. No production cut-outs, 3D model or rig exist yet. Changing anything below needs explicit approval from the owner.

## Role
The sidekick, the audience's curious friend. Curious, energetic, playful, relatable. Asks the questions and creates real-life context; adds humour and drama.

## Appearance
- Same stylised 3D cartoon style as Yusuf.
- Hair: voluminous dark-brown curly hair.
- Face: round thin black-framed glasses (signature), dark eyes, small smile, rosy cheeks.
- Default outfit: beige/cream hoodie with drawstrings over a white tee, grey over-ear headphones around the neck, black backpack, dark charcoal cargo joggers with side pockets, white sneakers with grey panels.

## Approved variations (from the storytelling board)
Outfits: Default (Hoodie), Jacket, T-Shirt, Sweater, Tech Vest, Lab Coat.
Expressions: 31 named emotions as listed on the storytelling board.

## Never
- Remove the glasses or headphones from the default look; straighten the hair; give him a goatee.
- Use Yusuf's voice for Aiman unless the owner explicitly selects that mode (see /voice).
