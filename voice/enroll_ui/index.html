Before you record

  • Quiet room with soft furnishings; switch off fans and air-conditioning if you can.
  • Keep the same mic and the same distance (about a hand-span, 15 to 20 cm) for every take.
  • Speak naturally, the way you talk on your channel: Bahasa Melayu with your normal English mix.
  • Record several styles: conversational, explaining, excited, calm, serious, humorous.
  • Avoid music, other voices, mouth clicks and heavy breathing into the mic.
  • Instant clone: 1 to 2 minutes of clean audio. Professional clone: at least 30 minutes, ideally 1 to 3 hours.
